# The releases of 2026-11-21

Cardamum moves to io-http 0.9.0, io-oauth 0.8.0 (dev), io-jmap 0.11.0, io-msgraph 0.10.0, io-gpeople 0.9.0, io-webdav 0.13.0, io-pimdir 0.19.1 and pimalaya-stream 0.4.1, io-proxy 0.3.0 following through the lock. A dependency move, no proposal, no behaviour change.

- **Features**: the io-* `client` feature, which named the blocking client, is now `std` (family rule cargo-006), `client` gating the coroutines. io-jmap, io-msgraph, io-gpeople and the dev-dependency io-oauth name `std` in place of `client`; io-webdav already named `std`; io-http keeps no feature, the backends enabling what it needs.
- **Lock**: `cargo update -p` on those crates moved them alone in Cargo.lock.
- **Code**: no source change.
- **Evidence**: `cargo clippy --locked --all-targets -- -D warnings` over the CI feature sets of .github/workflows/tests.yml, clean; `cargo test --locked --all-features`, 366 passed, 19 ignored (live suites); the two CI single-backend test steps green; `cargo fmt --check` clean.

io-pimdir 0.19.1 fixes the sync engine, which cardamum does not run: no CHANGELOG line. Capabilities moved: none.
