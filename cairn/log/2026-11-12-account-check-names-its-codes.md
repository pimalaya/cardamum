# account check names its codes

Each backend of `account check --json` carries `code` beside `error`, as Himalaya's and calendula's do, so a pimdir store's `store-busy` or `store-uncreated` reaches a script. `a_held_staging_lock_fails_the_check_as_busy` asserts it and failed on the result without the field. 113 tests pass.

Capabilities moved: commands ("account check fails when a backend fails").
